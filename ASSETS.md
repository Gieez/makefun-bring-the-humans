# Asset sources

- `assets/now-it-codes.jpg`: actual video frame at 2 seconds from make.fun's official Now It Codes ad, https://ads.make.fun/media/now-it-codes/9x16.mp4. The bounty explicitly permits make.fun's advertising assets. The frame is unaltered apart from resizing and JPEG encoding.
- `assets/lockup-on-dark.svg`, mark and wordmark variants: official make.fun press kit, https://make.fun/press/make-fun-press-kit.zip.
- `assets/space-grotesk-400.ttf` and `assets/space-grotesk-700.ttf`: Space Grotesk from Google Fonts, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk. License copied beside the fonts.
- `ad.png`: real Chromium screenshot of this repository's index.html, rendered at 1440px width. No generated image or simulated UI.
